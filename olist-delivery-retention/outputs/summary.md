# Olist delivery and retention summary

96,470 delivered orders from 93,350 customers and 2,959 sellers, Sep 2016 to Aug 2018. 6.8% arrived after the promised date.

## Late delivery and reviews
- Average review: 2.27 late vs 4.29 on time
- 1-2 star reviews: 62% late vs 9% on time (6.7x)
- 5-star reviews: 17% late vs 62% on time
  - On time: 89,443 orders, avg review 4.29, 9% negative
  - 1-3 days late: 1,852 orders, avg review 3.29, 32% negative
  - 4-7 days late: 1,748 orders, avg review 2.10, 68% negative
  - 8-14 days late: 1,446 orders, avg review 1.67, 80% negative
  - 15+ days late: 1,335 orders, avg review 1.72, 78% negative

## Late first delivery and repeat purchase (within 180 days of delivery)
- On time: 1,058 of 59,197 came back (1.79%)
- Late: 55 of 4,517 came back (1.22%)
- Late first orders had a 32% lower repeat rate (z = 2.82, p = 0.005; 95% CI for the gap 0.23 to 0.91 pts)

## Where lateness happens
- States with the highest late rate (300+ orders):
  - AL: 21.4% late, 397 orders, avg 1,833 km from seller, avg review 3.85
  - MA: 17.4% late, 717 orders, avg 2,100 km from seller, avg review 3.83
  - SE: 15.2% late, 335 orders, avg 1,648 km from seller, avg review 3.91
  - PI: 13.9% late, 476 orders, avg 1,957 km from seller, avg review 3.99
  - CE: 13.8% late, 1,279 orders, avg 2,186 km from seller, avg review 3.94
- By seller-to-customer distance:
  - <100 km: 4.4% late, 6.4 days to deliver
  - 100-499 km: 6.1% late, 11.5 days to deliver
  - 500-999 km: 7.1% late, 14.2 days to deliver
  - 1,000-1,999 km: 9.5% late, 17.9 days to deliver
  - 2,000+ km: 12.0% late, 21.1 days to deliver
- 622 sellers with 30+ orders, split into tenths by late rate. Worst 10%: 16.6% late, avg review 3.81; best 10%: 0.6% late, avg review 4.38
- Worst months: Mar 2018 (19.0%), Feb 2018 (14.1%), Nov 2017 (12.4%)
